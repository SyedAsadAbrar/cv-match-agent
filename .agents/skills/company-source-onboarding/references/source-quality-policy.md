# Source quality policy

Use sources in this order: official government register or open data; official public job portal; government-supported ecosystem; direct official company page; reviewed curated list.

Reject copied aggregators, search-result snippets as final evidence, anonymous lists, guessed domains, guessed ATS identifiers, login-protected sources, and sources that require bypassing access controls.

Store a structured derivative and its provenance. Do not copy full copyrighted pages. A record without an official domain remains a valid `candidate` and must stay disabled.
