# Country source map

- Netherlands: IND Public Register Work for sponsor evidence; classify engineering relevance separately.
- Ireland: Department of Enterprise employment-permit company listings for current and previous complete years.
- Germany: Make it in Germany listings and directly verified international technology employers.
- Denmark: Work in Denmark / Jobnet official English-language vacancy portal, then direct official employer pages for verified employers.
- European Union: EURES where technically accessible plus official company career pages in target markets.
- United Arab Emirates: Hub71 startup/job-board ecosystem and direct official UAE technology employer pages.
- Saudi Arabia: Monsha'at/MISA material where it identifies companies plus direct official Saudi digital-employer pages.

Always record the source publication/update date and retrieval date. Government ecosystem membership does not prove current hiring.
