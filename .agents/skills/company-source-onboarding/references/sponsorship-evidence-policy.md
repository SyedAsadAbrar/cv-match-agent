# Sponsorship evidence policy

An IND recognised-sponsor entry is `confirmed` company-level evidence for the Netherlands. Irish permit activity is `historical` evidence. A dated company statement may be `confirmed`; credible older evidence is `historical`; weak indications are `possible`.

Never downgrade or silently replace stronger evidence during refresh. Keep every source reference and effective date. Employer-level evidence does not prove a specific vacancy offers sponsorship; vacancy wording remains decisive.
